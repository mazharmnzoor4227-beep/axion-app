# Deep research

1. Break the question into 3–6 sub-questions.
2. For each, run a focused `web_search`; open the 2–3 best primary sources with `browse_url` (official sites, docs, reputable news), not just snippets.
3. Cross-check numbers across at least two sources; note disagreements.
4. Answer with: a direct answer first, then findings by sub-question, then a short "Sources" list of the URLs you actually opened.
5. Mark anything you could not verify as "unverified".
